# hey, i'm shabbir

<h3>✨ an on-chain analyst with a knack for Rust. ✨</h3>

finance background. engineering roots. currently building Recallr and spending a slightly unreasonable amount of time on AI.

i'm interested in what happens when agents meet real institutional workflows: investment research, memory, permissions, payments and the fairly unglamorous business of making the whole thing reliable.

## what i'm working on

- **Recallr** - the company i'm building.
- **[institutional ai fieldnotes](https://github.com/shabbiryk/institutional-ai-fieldnotes)** - a public notebook on AI agents and institutional infrastructure. primary sources, practical questions and things worth testing. AI-assisted and maintained by a scheduled research agent.
- **[Khan-Proxy](https://github.com/shabbiryk/Khan-Proxy)** - a project on AI tooling and gateways. implementation details and provenance live in the repository.

if you build tools for investment teams, agent infrastructure, memory or onchain finance, we'll probably have something to talk about.
